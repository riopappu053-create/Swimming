FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

What programs do you offer for beginners?
We offer Little Fins (ages 3–6) and Stroke Foundation (ages 7–12) as our beginner programs. Both focus on water safety, basic strokes, and building confidence in a fun, supportive environment.
How do I book a trial session?
You can book a free trial session by filling out the contact form on this page or by calling us directly at +1 305 555 0198. We'll match you with the right program and schedule.
What are your operating hours?
We're open Monday through Saturday, 6:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We're closed on Sundays, but private coaching sessions can be arranged by appointment.
Do you offer classes for adults?
Absolutely! Our Adult Masters program is designed for adults of all levels — from absolute beginners to former competitors. We also offer private coaching for adults.
How do I become a competitive swimmer at AquaPulse?
Swimmers progress through our pathway: Stroke Foundation → Junior Competitive → Elite Squad. Our coaches will assess your skill level and place you in the right group to help you reach your competitive goals.
What safety measures do you have in place?
We maintain certified lifeguards on duty at all times, modern facilities with safety equipment, and follow strict water quality standards. All coaches are CPR and first-aid certified.
