Our Journey

The AquaPulse Story

2012

Founded in Miami

AquaPulse started as a small community swim school with just 3 coaches and 40 students, driven by a vision to make swimming education world-class.

2016

Olympic Pool Partnership

Partnered with a 50m Olympic-standard facility, allowing us to expand programs and attract elite coaches and swimmers from across the region.

2020

Digital Transformation

Launched our online scheduling platform and virtual coaching modules, making AquaPulse accessible to a wider audience even during challenging times.

2026

2,600+ Swimmers & Growing

Today, AquaPulse is a premier swimming academy with 50+ coaches, 15 programs, and a thriving community of swimmers from beginners to national competitors.
